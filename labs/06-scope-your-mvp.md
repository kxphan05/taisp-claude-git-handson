# Lab 6: Scope your MVP (15 min)

Judges reward **"works"** over **"ambitious"**. An MVP (minimum viable product) is the smallest thing that solves one real problem for one real user.

## Fill this in with your team (or solo)

> **User:** Who exactly will use it? (e.g. "Year 1 TAISP scholars")
> **Pain point:** What annoys them today? (e.g. "Past-year notes vanish when seniors graduate")
> **Solution in one sentence:** ...
> **Must have (works by Day 1 night):** 1-3 features
> **Nice to have (only if time):** ...
> **Out of scope:** ...

## Example directions

- **Holy Grail:** searchable repository of notes and tips by module. MVP: upload, browse, search by module code and tag.
- **Opportunity Radar:** feed of internships, scholarships and talks for TAISP scholars. MVP: curated list with deadlines and tags.
- **Your own idea:** a module planner, teammate matcher, QR attendance, equipment booking...

## Use Claude to challenge your scope
Open Claude Code and prompt:
> "Here is my project plan: [paste]. I have 24 hours and beginner skills. What should I cut? What is the riskiest part?"

Then **you** decide. Claude advises; you own the plan.

## Judging rubric to keep in mind

| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Usefulness to TAISP | 40% |
| Working product and technical execution | 20% |
| Creativity | 20% |
| Presentation | 20% |

## Final checklist before submission
- [ ] Deployed link works
- [ ] GitHub repo has a README: problem, how it works, setup steps, handover notes
- [ ] Slides ready
- [ ] Everyone can explain the core code
- [ ] No secrets or real scholar data committed

## Checkpoint

- [ ] You have a one-sentence solution and a must-have list of 3 or fewer
